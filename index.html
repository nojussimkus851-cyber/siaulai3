01 — The company

Built for the non‑standard.

UAB „Reecon Steel“ was founded in 2001 in Šiauliai, Lithuania. Uniting people with long experience in technical decision-making and those who are young and enthusiastic, we have built a professional team that uses its knowledge and skills to solve problems.

Our core business is metal working. We produce standard products as well as whole series of tailor-made items. The basic requirements for our production are the highest quality, durability and price compatibility.

03 — Boat cradles

Boat cradles.

UAB „Reecon Steel“ manufactures boat cradles for sailboats and motorboats — Danish design, made in Šiauliai.

3T
  1. 01

    Capacity

    Cradles are divided by the weight of the boat: 3T, 6T, 12T, 15T, 18T, 25T and 35T.

  2. 02

    Material

    Made of black steel and hot-dip galvanized for long service outdoors.

  3. 03

    Stackable

    Our cradles are stackable — compact to store when the season turns.

  4. 04

    Hull fit

    Compatible with most hull shapes — for sailboats and motorboats alike.

  5. 05

    Danish design

    Boat cradles of Danish design, produced and galvanized for harbours and marinas.

Additional parts

Mobile stander

Pressfoots
